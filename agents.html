For real estate agents

Send one link. Your buyer lands on a page that already knows them.

This page explains the referral flow Meadowbrook Mortgage Demo builds for its agent partners: a short link per loan officer, an optional parameter that carries your name, and attribution that follows the buyer all the way to a submitted application.

How the flow works

  1. Step one

    Get a vanity link per loan officer

    Each loan officer has a short redirect: /sarah, /marcus, or /jordan. It's short enough to read aloud and to retype correctly if a buyer mistypes it.

  2. Step two

    Add your name to the link

    Append ?agent=YourName to any of those links and the landing page greets the buyer with both your name and the loan officer's. Spaces in a name should be URL-encoded, for example %20 or +.

  3. Step three

    Attribution rides to the application

    When the buyer taps Apply, both the agent name and loan officer slug carry forward into hidden fields on the application, so the submitted lead always records who sent it.

A worked example

A fictional agent, Dana Whitfield, refers a buyer to Sarah Chen. The link Dana sends looks like this:

/start?agent=Dana%20Whitfield&lo=sarah

Open the worked example to see the personalized landing page it produces, then try editing the agent and lo values in the address bar.

Encoding your name in a link

Use %20 or + in place of spaces, and stick to standard letters wherever you can. Most phone keyboards and text apps will encode this automatically when you paste a link, but it's worth double-checking before you send.

What you get out of it

A co-branded handoff

Your buyer sees your name on the same page as the loan officer's, instead of landing on a generic homepage that makes no mention of who referred them.

No login required

There's no portal, no password and nothing to install. The whole flow is a link you can send from any phone.

Works from a phone, mid-showing

Pages are static and load quickly on a cellular connection, so sending a link between showings takes seconds, not minutes.

Attribution you can trust

Every submitted application carries the agent and loan officer values forward, so a referral is never lost between the text message and the CRM.
