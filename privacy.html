Privacy policy

This is a sample document, not a real policy

Meadowbrook Mortgage Demo is a fictional business created as a portfolio demonstration by 1MR LLC. It is not a real financial services firm. The text below is an illustrative sample showing the shape a privacy policy takes on a site of this kind. It is not legal advice, it is not an enforceable policy, and it governs nothing.

This demo transmits nothing. The application form is a non-functional mock with no destination. No submission is sent, stored or forwarded. Please do not enter real personal or financial information anywhere on this site.

Last reviewed: September 2025.

What a real site would collect

A working version of this site would handle two broad categories of information.

Information you provide

Whatever you type into the application form or send by email, text or phone. On a mortgage site that typically includes your name, email address, phone number and a description of what you are trying to do, plus the referring agent and loan officer carried in the link you followed.

Information collected automatically

Standard web analytics: pages viewed, approximate location derived from IP address, referring URL, device and browser type, and interactions such as tapping a call, text or apply button. This is aggregate behaviour data, not file content.

Cookies and similar technologies

A live deployment would set analytics cookies through Google Analytics 4 to distinguish one visit from another and to measure whether the referral flow works. It would not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Doing so affects measurement only; the pages themselves continue to work, because none of the content on this site depends on a cookie.

Third-party processors

A real version of this site would share information with a small number of service providers, each processing it only to provide their service:

  • HubSpot — CRM and form handling. Application submissions, including the referring agent and loan officer, would be stored there.
  • Google Analytics 4 — aggregate usage measurement. No form field contents are sent to analytics.
  • Cloudflare Pages — static hosting and content delivery, which processes request metadata such as IP address in the ordinary course of serving a page.

Information would not be sold, rented or traded. It would be disclosed to others only where required by law or with your consent.

Retention

A real firm would keep enquiry records only as long as needed for the purpose they were collected for, and for any period required by applicable financial services record-keeping rules, then delete or anonymise them. Analytics data would be kept for a bounded retention window configured in the analytics account.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information is held about you, to receive a copy of it, to have inaccuracies corrected, to have it deleted, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to opt out of marketing messages at any time. A real firm would tell you how to exercise those rights and would not treat you differently for doing so.

Security

A live site would be served over HTTPS, would limit access to submitted enquiries to the people who need it, and would rely on its CRM provider's controls for data at rest. No system is perfectly secure, and any honest policy says so.

Children

A site of this kind is directed at adults and would not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this document

A real policy would be updated from time to time, with the revision date at the top changed to match.
