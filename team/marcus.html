About Marcus

Marcus works with the buyer who already owns a home and is trying to figure out the math of moving up: how much equity actually converts to a down payment, whether to sell first or buy first, and what a bridge of a few months does to a monthly budget. It's a different set of tradeoffs than a first purchase, and he'd rather spend an extra call running scenarios than have a buyer guess.

Renovation lending is the other half of his work, and it's the part he finds genuinely interesting: financing that accounts for the home a buyer is about to remodel, not just the home as it sits today. That means coordinating timelines with contractors and appraisers in a way a standard purchase never requires, and Marcus treats that coordination as his job, not the buyer's.

Agent partners who send Marcus a listing with "needs work, priced accordingly" in the notes get a fast, honest read on whether the numbers can work before the buyer falls in love with the kitchen. He'd rather deliver that news in week one than week five.

Expect direct, numbers-first conversations, a realistic timeline instead of an optimistic one, and a loan officer who answers the "can we actually afford this" question straight, even when it's not the answer someone wanted.
