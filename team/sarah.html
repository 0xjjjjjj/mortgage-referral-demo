About Sarah

Sarah spends most of her week talking buyers through the first mortgage they've ever taken out, and she's built her whole approach around the questions that come up the most: what does "under contract" actually mean, why does the lender care about two years of pay stubs, and why did the estimated payment change between the pre-approval and the final numbers. None of it is complicated once someone walks through it slowly, which is most of what she does.

Condos are her other specialty, and for good reason: they carry a layer of underwriting that single-family homes don't, from HOA budgets to owner- occupancy ratios, and a file can stall for weeks if nobody catches an issue early. Sarah reviews condo association paperwork before an offer is even written whenever she can, so buyers aren't blindsided six weeks into escrow.

She works closely with agent partners across DuPage County, and she asks for the same thing from every one of them: send the buyer over as soon as they start seriously looking, not after they've already found "the one." Buyers who get a pre-approval conversation early make stronger offers, and agents who send buyers early get a partner who already knows the file when an offer needs to move fast.

If you're referred to Sarah, expect a first conversation that's mostly questions on her end, a written summary of what to expect next, and straightforward answers even when the honest answer is "it depends."
