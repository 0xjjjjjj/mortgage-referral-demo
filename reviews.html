Why the average is 4.8

Five of the six testimonials above are rated five stars and one is rated four, which is 29 stars across 6 reviews, or 4.83 — 4.8 to one decimal place. That is the same figure carried in this site's aggregateRating structured data, with a reviewCount of 6. A schema rating that disagrees with the reviews a visitor can actually count on the page is both a validator warning and, on a real site, a policy problem.
